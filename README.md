# Akademia: Journal of Education & Innovation

[**Akademia: Journal of Education & Innovation**](https://journal.nafenzi.or.id/akademia) is a peer-reviewed academic journal dedicated to advancing research, scholarship, and knowledge in **education, learning, teaching, educational technology, innovation, and related fields**.

Akademia provides a scholarly platform for **researchers, educators, academics, practitioners, policymakers, and education professionals** to share rigorous research, theoretical perspectives, innovative practices, and evidence-based insights that contribute to the continuous development of education.

The journal welcomes scholarly contributions addressing both theoretical and applied dimensions of education and innovation. Its scope may include **educational policy, curriculum and instruction, pedagogy, learning sciences, teacher education, educational leadership and management, assessment and evaluation, educational technology, digital learning, artificial intelligence in education, inclusive education, lifelong learning, educational innovation, and emerging trends in education**.

Akademia encourages research that bridges **academic scholarship and educational practice**, providing knowledge that can support educators, institutions, policymakers, and communities in responding to the evolving challenges and opportunities of education in a rapidly changing world.

As part of the broader **Nafenzi academic and scholarly publishing ecosystem**, Akademia is supported by Nafenzi Institute, Nafenzi Press, and the Open Publishing System (OPS).

# Open Publishing System (OPS) | Scholarly • Open • Trusted

[**Open Publishing System (OPS)**](https://ops.nxi.or.id/) by [**Nafenzi eXperience Identifier (NXI)**](https://nxi.or.id/) is a modern scholarly publishing ecosystem designed to support the **management, publication, discovery, and dissemination of scientific knowledge**.

OPS brings together journals, researchers, institutions, scholarly content, metadata, and research-related services within an interconnected digital environment.

Built around the principles of **Scholarly • Open • Trusted**, OPS is designed to promote:

* **Scholarly** — supporting rigorous academic communication and research dissemination.
* **Open** — encouraging accessible, discoverable, and shareable scholarly knowledge.
* **Trusted** — strengthening the integrity, reliability, transparency, and traceability of academic information.

OPS serves as the technological foundation connecting the different components of the Nafenzi scholarly ecosystem and enabling academic publications to be managed and disseminated through modern digital infrastructure.

# Our Vision

Akademia is founded on the belief that **education is continuously shaped by research, innovation, technology, and the changing needs of society**.

The journal seeks to foster a scholarly environment where researchers and practitioners can exchange ideas, examine emerging educational challenges, and develop evidence-based approaches to improving learning and educational systems.

By promoting rigorous and relevant scholarship, Akademia aims to support the development of **innovative, inclusive, adaptive, and sustainable education**.

The journal is committed to ensuring that research can be **conducted with integrity, communicated responsibly, discovered openly, and translated into knowledge that contributes to meaningful educational improvement**.
